What am I looking at?

Three computer programs that buy and sell stocks on their own, using practice accounts with fake money. Each one tries a different approach. The question: can any of them beat simply buying the whole stock market (an index fund called SPY) and holding it?

Regime

Slow and steady. Reads the overall market mood once a day and adjusts. Most days it just holds the market.
